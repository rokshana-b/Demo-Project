# Business requirements

All requirements below are invented for demonstration.

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance note |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-001 | The quote form shall collect a garaging ZIP, vehicle year/make/model, and driver date of birth. | High | Required fields are identified and validated before estimate generation. |
| REQ-002 | The quote form shall explain missing or invalid required values next to the relevant field. | High | The user can identify and correct each invalid value. |
| REQ-003 | A valid submission shall display an estimated premium and a unique fictional quote reference. | High | Both appear on the estimate screen. |
| REQ-004 | The estimate shall show an eligible safe-driver discount and multi-vehicle discount in its breakdown. | Medium | The fictional sample rules are applied once each when eligible. |
| REQ-005 | The user shall be able to attach a supporting document in PDF, JPG, or PNG format up to 5 MB. | Medium | Unsupported types and files over the limit receive a clear prompt. |
| REQ-006 | The user shall be able to review the entered details before leaving the quote flow. | Low | A review screen shows the entered vehicle and driver details. |
