# TC-003: Cancelled Policy Is Not Eligible for Renewal

**Requirement:** REQ-003  
**Priority:** Medium  
**Type:** Business rule

## Preconditions
- A fictional cancelled policy exists in the test dataset.
- User has policy-view permission.

## Steps
1. Open the policy summary.
2. Navigate to renewal eligibility.
3. Review the eligibility result and reason.

## Expected results
- Eligibility is `Not Eligible`.
- The reason indicates that the policy is cancelled.
