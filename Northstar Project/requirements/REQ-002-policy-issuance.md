# REQ-002: Issue a Policy

**Status:** Ready for review  
**Priority:** High  
**Area:** Policy

## User story
As an Underwriter, I want to issue an approved quote so that it becomes an active policy.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Only an eligible quote can be issued.
2. A quote missing required approval cannot be issued.
3. Successful issuance creates a policy reference.
4. A Service Representative can view the issued policy summary but cannot issue the policy.
5. The system records the issuance date in the policy record.
